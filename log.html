Personal Log

Wednesday 11 February — Viewing Day

13:00 During the viewing, I was present with the realtor when the previous tenant/roommate entered and said, “What are you doing in my home?”

The realtor said he had rung the doorbell and received no answer. She claimed she had not been informed of any visit. The next day, when the realtor called to apologize, she made demands that I not use certain items in the apartment.

That evening, I used the toilet and there was toilet paper. I received the key and went back home. I decided to give it a trial period and checked with the landlord. This was approved.

Later that day, the landlord informed me that the roommate did not want me to use the fridge, air fryer, washing machine, couch, or coffee table, as she claimed they were hers. The couch and coffee table occupy approximately 75% of the communal room and are co-owned with the previous roommate.

Thursday 12 February (Morning)

Met Kim Phillips at my old home.

Then went to Nijmegen to sleep there. I brought dry food that did not require a fridge and a cool box.

When I entered my room for the first night (12 → 13 February), I found that all the bulbs had been removed from the ceiling light fixture. I spent my first evening in the dark.

Friday 13 February

The next morning, all the toilet paper was gone. I needed to use the toilet, so I had to go to the shop. I bought toilet paper and also a lamp, which later turned out to be the wrong type.

I left a handwritten note for my roommate (I cannot type or print due to a stroke). In the note, I questioned the division of communal space and the rules regarding fridge usage. I asked her to remove personal items cluttering the communal space. I stated that I had been told by the landlord that a fridge was present. I noted that there is only space for one washing machine and that I also need fridge space. I asked whether the couch could be removed and whether the communal setup could be restored.

She did not acknowledge the note in writing. I later spoke to her directly because she had not responded.

In conversation, she stated that she is a strict vegetarian for Hindu religious reasons and therefore has difficulty sharing crockery. I explained that I am vegetarian/vegan as well (for cholesterol reasons), so there should be no conflict regarding food type.

Regarding the fridge, she stated:

  • The old fridge broke.
  • She bought a new fridge with the previous roommate.
  • She must check with the previous roommate before allowing me to use it.
  • She informed the landlord about this.
  • She refused my requests.

After buying the wrong lamp, I left another handwritten note offering it to her if she could use it. This note was also not acknowledged. I stored the lamp in the single kitchen closet allocated to me. She has seven closets and two drawers in the kitchen. I have not formally addressed this imbalance yet.

At midday on 13 February, I went to my old home, which is being emptied for renovation. After working hard, I experienced a bad spell and stayed there overnight. I had planned to return in time to switch the cooling elements in my cool box. Because I could not return in time, two meals spoiled and had to be discarded. The freezer space available to me remains limited to cooling elements only.

Saturday 14 February

14:08 Returned to Nijmegen. I believe someone had been in my room. I had created a door seal because there was no key for the lock. The seal was broken.

Later that day, I contacted the landlord to verify the fridge replacement story and to check whether they had entered the room (for example, to check the light).

I called the landlord; they denied entering. If that is correct, only one other person has access to the building. Nothing was taken.

The landlord further stated they had no knowledge of the old fridge breaking or of any new fridge being purchased. I sent a photo; it is clearly a new fridge. The landlord’s account and my roommate’s account are mutually inconsistent.

After I had already spoken with her about my earlier note, I later found my handwritten note in the trash. She had not acknowledged it in writing and had only responded verbally to specific questions. Nothing was resolved. Seeing it discarded felt odd, possibly because the underlying issues remained unresolved.

Sunday 15 February (Morning)

The fridge shelf designated for me was filled by my roommate. I cleared it again. I had bought tomato paste earlier and wanted to store it properly since I have a designated fridge shelf.

Went to a party with family. It was snowing when I returned at 23:49.

I am writing things down in detail now.

The apartment is warm now. It had been consistently 16.5°C before. I do not know how to operate the thermostat. This will be a more comfortable night.

I feel fairly certain now that the roommate has lied, entered my room, and is generally being unreasonable and trying to get me to leave.

I checked the nanny cam I set up. I was the only one entering and leaving the room.

End of log (15 February).
